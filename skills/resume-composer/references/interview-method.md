# Interview Method

Standalone mode only. In injected mode the evidence already exists and this file does not apply.

The user typically arrives with one vague sentence: 「我有幫忙處理系上活動」. The interview turns that into facts they can stand behind in a real interview. It replaces the separate evidence-curation step, so the same discipline applies here.

## Unit of work

Handle one bounded episode at a time: one internship assignment, project, competition, research effort, club activity, volunteer activity, or job episode. If the user's input mixes episodes, take the clearest one first and come back to the others. Never merge unrelated episodes into one entry to make it look larger.

## Three questions, in order

Ask them one at a time and wait for the answer. Do not present them as a form.

1. **場景** — 這是什麼活動、在哪裡、大概什麼時候、持續多久？
2. **你自己做了什麼** — 這件事裡哪一部分是你自己負責的，哪一部分是團隊或別人做的？
3. **有沒有可數的東西** — 有沒有任何數字：幾個人、幾週、幾篇、多少預算、多少參與者、前後有什麼變化？

Question 2 is the one that decides whether the entry survives a real interview. Do not skip it because the user sounds confident.

## Contribution tests

- Preserve the user's verb strength. 「協助」 does not become 「主導」.
- Separate 「我做的」 from 「我們做的」. Attribute a team result to the team unless individual causality is supported.
- Treat tools as methods used, not proof of proficiency.
- Treat participation as evidence of exposure, not mastery.
- When ownership stays ambiguous, write the observed action and record the ownership question in `unknowns`.

## Quantification discipline

This is the product's stated promise to the user. It is not negotiable to make a bullet look better.

1. **Something countable exists** — ask until you have the number: 幾人、幾週、幾篇、多少預算、成長多少。Use the number the user gives, unchanged.
2. **No single number, but a rate or range exists** — use the rate or range: 「每週 2 篇」、「約 30–40 人」. A range must come from the user, not from your estimate.
3. **Nothing countable can be established** — keep the sentence qualitative, and say so plainly: 「這段沒有數字，我們就寫成質性描述，面試時你講得出過程就夠了。」

Never fill a gap with a plausible-looking figure, a rounded guess, an industry benchmark, or a placeholder such as `XX%`. A qualitative bullet the user can defend beats a quantified bullet they cannot.

If the user offers a number they are unsure of, ask how they know it. If they cannot say, mark it unresolved rather than printing it.

## Confirmation

After drafting the wording for an episode, show it and ask exactly one question:

> 這樣寫有沒有超過事實？

- Affirmed — assign the next `chat-N` ID and move to the next episode.
- Corrected — revise and ask again. The affirmation must apply to the final wording.
- Unanswered — the entry stays out of the final draft. Silence, enthusiasm, or continued conversation is not confirmation.

If a later turn adds facts to an episode already affirmed, keep the earlier wording, add the new facts, and re-confirm the changed entry.
