# Story Method

## STAR construction

- **Situation:** only the setting needed to understand the problem.
- **Task:** the user's actual responsibility, not the team's whole mission.
- **Action:** concrete personal decisions, steps, tools, methods, and collaboration.
- **Result:** supported observable outcome. If no outcome is known, say what was delivered or learned without claiming impact.

Aim for a natural 60–90 second spoken answer. Use first person only for the user's contribution. Use “我們” for team outcomes and identify the boundary.

## Follow-up points

Flag claims an interviewer may reasonably probe: why a method was chosen, the user's exact role, how quality was checked, what changed, what failed, and what the user would do differently. Do not manufacture answers.

## Honesty boundary

For every unresolved claim, provide a truthful limit such as “我負責整理與提醒，最後決策由總召做” or “沒有保留錯誤減少數據，因此只能說有發現缺漏.” A polished story cannot exceed the confirmed source.
