# Direction Hypothesis Method

## Signal lanes

Keep these lanes distinct: activities the user enjoys doing, work values, preferred contexts, capability clues, content domains, hard constraints, and uncertainties. Preserve the source wording or injected snapshot label for every rationale.

## Hypothesis quality

A useful direction describes a family of work rather than one employer or one narrow title. Prefer combinations of two or more signal lanes. Keep the set broad enough to include different functions when evidence supports them.

For each hypothesis provide:

- a plain-language title;
- two or more traceable reasons when available;
- one uncertainty that could change the hypothesis;
- one 30-minute micro-task that samples the actual activity, not merely reading career advice;
- an attention allocation.

Do not infer marketing from a language interest, engineering from one tool, management from organizing one event, or suitability from enthusiasm. A capability clue is neither a self-rating nor confirmed evidence.

## Validation actions

Use a concrete action with a time box, artifact, and observation prompt. Examples include drafting one screen flow, analyzing ten rows of public data, outlining a user interview, or rewriting one confusing explanation. Do not require an application, purchase, or contact with a third party.
